        How to play

        Find Pi

        Pi, a goldendoodle, is hiding in a mystery country. Type any country in the box and press Guess.

        BraGuess

        Warmer means closer

        Each guess lights up on the globe. Red is close to Pi, blue is far.

        Brazil 6,140 mi Spain 1,320 mi Greece 310 mi
        farclose

        Clues help you sniff

        You get 6 guesses. The case file holds three clues: population, flag colors and continent. They stay hidden until you tap Reveal.

        Revealing a clue or using a trail hint (Pi's paw print near his hiding spot) uses one hint. You get 3 free hints every day; after that each costs 20 treats.

        Racing a rival

        You both hunt the same countries. Find Pi and you move to the next one; miss 6 times and that country is skipped. First to the target wins.
