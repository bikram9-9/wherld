Where's Pi

Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Where's Pi is a free geography game at wherespi.com. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • If you play as a guest: the nickname you choose, your game results (rank, treats, wins and losses, scores, outfits), and a random key stored in your browser that links you to them.
  • If you create an account: your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with us. We do not get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • If you turn on two-step sign-in: a record that it is set up. The code secret lives in your authenticator app and our sign-in provider.
  • Your browser keeps your sign-in session and a few game preferences in local storage so you stay signed in and your daily streak is remembered.
  • Usage and error statistics: which pages and game modes are used, game results such as number of guesses, how fast pages load, error messages from the site, the website that sent you here, your device type, browser, language and time zone, and a random visitor key stored in your browser so repeat visits can be counted. We use no cookies; a random visitor key is kept in your browser's local storage. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we collect only error reports. Our own records are deleted after 180 days.
  • Session recordings: to find and fix bugs, we record how the site is used during your visit: clicks, taps, scrolling, what appears on screen (including the globe), the countries you type, browser console messages and the timing of requests to our game server. Password and email fields are hidden in recordings, and so is your two-step sign-in key. Recordings and game events are linked to your nickname and game stats, and, if you are signed in, to your account ID and email address. Recordings are kept for 30 days. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, nothing is recorded.

How we use it

To run the game: to sign you in, save your progress, match you with other players, and show nicknames and scores on leaderboards. Usage and error statistics and session recordings are used only to keep the site working and to improve the game. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Who processes it

Accounts and game data are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. The website is served by GitHub Pages. Google handles "Continue with Google" sign-in. If you buy treats, Stripe processes the payment: your card details go to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us; we keep a record of which treat pack you bought and when. Resend delivers our account emails, such as sign-up confirmations and password resets, so it receives your email address for that purpose. PostHog stores our usage statistics and session recordings, and Sentry stores error reports (what went wrong, your browser and device type, and your account ID or nickname); both host this data in the United States. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Email us from the address on your account and we will delete your account and game data. Clearing your browser removes guest data stored there.

Children

Where's Pi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
